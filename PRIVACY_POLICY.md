# Privacy Policy - Anigame Clan Donation Tracker Bot

**Last Updated: October 4, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

The Anigame Clan Donation Tracker Bot (the "Bot") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

By using the Bot, you agree to this Privacy Policy and acknowledge that your data will be processed as described herein. This policy complies with Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Developer Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

The Bot collects and stores the following information:

### Automatically Collected:
- **Discord User ID**: Your unique Discord identifier (for linking to Anigame names)
- **Discord Username**: Your display name in the Discord server
- **Anigame Username**: Your in-game clan member name
- **Damage Values**: Clan standing damage figures from Anigame's `.br cl` command
- **Donation Amounts**: Calculated donation values based on damage tiers
- **Payment Records**: Amounts paid toward donations
- **Timestamps**: When donations were recorded and payments were made
- **Message IDs**: References to Discord messages containing donation data

### No Additional Collection:
- The Bot does NOT collect direct messages or private conversations
- The Bot does NOT collect payment method information
- The Bot does NOT collect personal identifying information beyond Discord/Anigame names
- The Bot does NOT use cookies or tracking technologies

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for:
- **Donation Tracking**: Recording and calculating clan member donation obligations
- **Payment Logging**: Tracking donation payments received
- **Authorization**: Determining who can access administrative commands
- **Reporting**: Generating donation statements and leaderboards for authorized users
- **Data Management**: Storing and updating donation records

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- **Storage Location**: Data is stored in a `data.json` file on the server running the Bot
- **Access Control**: Only Discord server administrators and authorized users can access donation data
- **No Encryption**: Data is stored in plain text; server administrators should secure their files
- **No Cloud Storage**: Data remains on your local server and is not uploaded to external services
- **Backup Responsibility**: Server administrators are responsible for backing up Bot data

## 5. Who Has Access to Your Information

Your donation information can be viewed by:
- **You**: You can view your own donation and payment status via the `-donations` command
- **Server Administrators**: Full access to all Bot data and commands
- **Authorized Users**: Those granted permission by administrators via the `-authorize` command
- **Clan Members**: May see aggregate leaderboard data via `-donations_all` (if run by admins)

The Bot does NOT share your information with:
- Third-party services or platforms
- Anigame or any external game services
- Discord (beyond normal Discord API usage)
- Other servers or users without authorization

## 6. Data Retention

- **Retention Period**: Data is retained indefinitely until manually deleted by a server administrator
- **Deletion**: Administrators can use the `-clear_totals` command to permanently delete all donation and payment records
- **History**: The Bot maintains a payment log for accountability; this log is deleted when totals are cleared

## 7. Third-Party Services

The Bot integrates with:
- **Discord**: For message sending and user identification (subject to Discord's Privacy Policy)
- **Anigame**: For reading publicly displayed clan standings (no data is sent to Anigame)

The Bot does NOT use analytics services, advertising platforms, or data brokers.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request your donation and payment records from your server administrator
- **Correction**: Dispute or correct inaccurate donation records
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your donation data by asking your server administrator to use the `-unlink` command or `-clear_totals`
- **Opt-Out**: Leave the Discord server or request the Bot be removed

In the event of unauthorized access to Bot data:
- The Bot creators will not be liable for damages
- Server administrators should immediately review their server security
- Local backups should be checked for unauthorized modifications
- The Discord server should be audited for compromised tokens or permissions

## 11. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the Bot, please contact your server administrator immediately.

Continued use of the Bot after the notice period indicates acceptance of any changes.
